📐 Physics Formulas Reference
Newton's Second Law:
F_{net} = m \cdot a
Net Force with Friction:
F_{net} = F_{applied} - F_{friction}
Acceleration:
a = \frac{F_{net}}{m}
Velocity Kinematics:
v(t) = v_0 + a \cdot t
Position Kinematics:
x(t) = x_0 + v_0 t + \frac{1}{2} a t^2
Kinetic Energy:
KE = \frac{1}{2} m v^2
Momentum:
p = m \cdot v
Work Done:
W = F_{net} \cdot d

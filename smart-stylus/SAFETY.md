# Safety & Electrical Guidelines

The Smart Stylus is strictly a low-voltage (3.3V battery-powered) passive input device.

- **No Electrical Output**: Zero electrical feedback, stimulation, or current output to the user.
- **Low Voltage Circuitry**: Powered by a single 3.7V LiPo battery regulated down to 3.3V DC.
- **Classroom Safe**: Designed for low-power BLE wireless operation.
